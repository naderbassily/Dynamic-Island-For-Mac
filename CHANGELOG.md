# Changelog

## 2.1.0

- New **floating pill** look (fully rounded, like iPhone), vertically centred in the menu bar
- **Size** choice: Small, Medium, Large. **Style** choice: floating pill or attached to the top edge
- Idle pill groups the clock and weather in the middle
- New app icon
- Version number shown in the menu bar menu and in Settings ▸ About
- **Make Shareable App** creates a universal `.dmg` and `.zip` (Apple silicon + Intel) you can AirDrop, email or upload
- Installer saves a `build-log.txt` if something goes wrong
- GitHub releases now include a `.dmg` as well as a `.zip`

## 2.0.0

Works great on **MacBook** as well as Mac mini / iMac / Mac Studio.

- **MacBook notch support:** the island wraps around the camera notch and keeps content on either side
- **Charging and battery:** plugged in / unplugged banners with %, time to full and adapter watts, low battery at 20% and 10%, fully charged
- Low Power Mode and overheating banners
- Volume and Caps Lock HUDs
- Orange microphone-in-use dot
- Bluetooth banners for AirPods, headphones, keyboards and mice, plus their battery levels
- "Download complete" banners
- **Shelf** tab: drop files on the island, drag them out, AirDrop them; clipboard history (skips passwords); recent downloads
- **Timer** tab: quick timers and Pomodoro focus/break cycles, with a live countdown in the pill
- **System** tab: CPU, memory, disk, network, battery or uptime, device batteries
- **Shortcuts** tab: run and pin your Apple Shortcuts
- Weather (Open-Meteo) in the header and in the idle pill
- Idle pill shows the clock and weather; hides in full-screen apps
- **Integrations:** `dynamicisland://` URL scheme, local web API on port 7787, `island` command-line tool
- Bigger Settings window with switches for everything

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Now Playing for Apple Music, Spotify and YouTube / YouTube Music in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Safari
- Calendar with countdowns, one-click Join for Teams, Zoom, Google Meet and Webex, and a pop-up before meetings
- Spotlight-style search (⌥ Space) for apps, files, Google and YouTube
- New-message banners and unread counts for Mail, Outlook, Teams, WhatsApp, Messages, Slack, Telegram, Discord, Signal and more
- Sender and subject for new Apple Mail emails
- Settings window: turn features on/off, choose apps to watch, sounds, hover behaviour, multi-display
- Launch at login
