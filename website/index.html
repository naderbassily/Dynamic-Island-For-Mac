        FAQ
        
Questions, answered.

      

      

        
Is it really free?
Yes. No subscription, no account, no ads.

        
Why does macOS warn me the first time I open it?
The app isn’t notarized by Apple yet (that needs a paid developer account). Right-click the app ▸ Open ▸ Open once, or click Open Anyway in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security. After that it opens normally.

        
Is it safe? What data does it collect?
Nothing leaves your Mac except album artwork and the weather (sent with your approximate location to Open-Meteo). There’s no analytics or tracking, and the source code is open for anyone to read.

        
Which Macs does it work on?
Any Mac on macOS 14 Sonoma or later — MacBook Air & Pro (with or without a notch), Mac mini, iMac, Mac Studio, Mac Pro, and external displays. Apple silicon and Intel.

        
How do my Teams meetings show up?
The island reads macOS Calendar. Add your work account in System Settings ▸ Internet Accounts ▸ Microsoft Exchange and turn on Calendars — then every Teams link gets a Join button.

        
Can it show the text of my Teams or WhatsApp messages?
macOS doesn’t let apps read other apps’ notifications, so the island shows how many new messages arrived (from the Dock badges). Apple Mail is the exception — you’ll see sender and subject.

        
YouTube shows the title but won’t pause.
In Chrome, Edge or Brave turn on View ▸ Developer ▸ Allow JavaScript from Apple Events. In Safari: Develop ▸ Allow JavaScript from Apple Events.

        
How do I uninstall it?
Click the capsule icon in the menu bar ▸ Quit, then drag the app from Applications to the Trash.
